Deleting or overriding a tag leaves its previous manifest in the repository, still pullable by its digest. Delete the manifests no tag points to: they stop being pullable by digest, then the layers only they used are freed.

Do not run this while an image is being pushed by digest. To delete only the layers no manifest uses, use Orphan Layers below.
